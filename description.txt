# Lava Sponge Mod

A Minecraft mod for NeoForge that adds unique sponge-based blocks with interesting mechanics.

## Features

- **Lava Sponge** - Absorbs lava in the world, turning it back into air. Right-click to place and it will automatically suck up nearby lava.
- **Wet Lava Sponge** - The damp version that can dry back into a regular Lava Sponge.
- **Prissian** - A beautiful prismarine-style block for building.
- **Magmarine** - A magical marine-themed block craftable with glowstone.
- **Magma Lantern** - A bright lantern that provides light.

## Crafting Recipes

- **Magmarine**: Combine prismarine shards, glowstone, and other materials to create this unique block.
- **Magma Lantern**: Crafted similar to sea lanterns but with magma-related materials.

## Installation

Requires NeoForge. Download from CurseForge or Modrinth.
